# Privacy

Cabinet is a client for your own RomM server. It collects nothing about you
and sends nothing anywhere except to that server.

**What Cabinet stores on your device.** The address of your RomM server,
the token RomM issues when you approve the device, your settings, and the
games and saves you choose to keep for offline play. The token lives in the
system keychain. Nothing here leaves the device except to talk to your
server.

**What Cabinet sends to your RomM server.** Your library requests, the
save states and in-game saves you make, and which games you play, so RomM
can show your recent games on other devices. RomM is software you run; its
data is yours and stays wherever you host it.

**What Cabinet sends anywhere else.** Nothing. There are no analytics, no
crash reporting service, no advertising, and no accounts other than the
one on your own server. Cabinet has no servers of its own.

**Local network.** Cabinet asks for local network access so a phone can be
a controller for an Apple TV or a Mac on the same Wi-Fi, and so it can
reach a RomM server by a local address. That traffic stays on your network.

**Notifications and Live Activities.** Used only to tell you when a
platform finishes downloading. Nothing about them is sent to anyone.

**Children.** Cabinet ships no game content of its own and has no accounts
of its own; the age rating reflects that.
